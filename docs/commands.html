Daily Picks
3 commands
/picks
Today's curated picks
Get the current day's stock and crypto picks with entry price, profit target, and stop loss.
What you get
A fresh list of short-term stock picks and crypto picks (if enabled), each with an entry zone, profit target, and stop-loss level. Arrives automatically every morning before market open — use this command any time to pull it on-demand.
Examples
/picks Shows today's picks
Tip
💡 Crypto picks only appear if crypto is toggled on. Use /crypto on to enable them.
/weekly
Weekly performance recap
See how the week's picks performed — win/loss stats, best and worst picks, and a market outlook.
What you get
A ranked list of the week's picks sorted by performance, a win rate summary, and a brief forward-looking market note for the coming week. Sent automatically every weekend, or pull it any time with this command.
Examples
/weekly Shows this week's recap
/regime
Market regime snapshot
See the current market environment — bull, bear, or neutral — based on VIX and momentum signals.
What you get
A one-line summary of the current market regime (BULL / BEAR / NEUTRAL), the live VIX reading, a momentum indicator, and a brief context note. Useful for understanding the broader backdrop before acting on any pick.
Examples
/regime Shows current market regime + VIX
Tip
💡 In a BEAR regime, picks are more conservative. The bot adjusts stop-loss levels automatically based on regime.

Price Alerts
3 commands
/alert
Set a price alert
Get notified the moment a stock or crypto hits your target price.
How to use
Type the command, then describe the alert in natural language or use a ticker directly. The bot understands company names and will confirm the ticker before setting the alert.
Examples
/alert NVDA 1000 Alert when NVDA hits $1,000
/alert BTC 100000 Alert when Bitcoin hits $100k
/alert Bot will ask for ticker and price
Tip
💡 You can say company names like "Apple" or "Nvidia" — the bot will look up the right ticker automatically.
/alerts
View active alerts
See all your currently active price alerts in one place.
What you get
A list of every alert you've set, showing the ticker, your target price, and the current price so you can see how close each one is.
Examples
/alerts Shows all active alerts with current prices
/unalert
Remove an alert
Cancel a price alert you no longer need.
How to use
Tap /unalert and the bot will show your active alerts as tappable buttons. Select the one you want to remove.
Examples
/unalert Shows a list to pick from and remove
/unalert NVDA Remove alert for NVDA directly

Watchlist
3 commands
/watchlist
View your watchlist
See all tickers you're watching, with current prices and daily changes.
What you get
A live snapshot of every ticker on your watchlist — current price, day's change, and percentage move. Great for a quick morning scan before deciding which picks to act on.
Examples
/watchlist Shows all watched tickers with live prices
Tip
💡 The watchlist is different from alerts — it doesn't notify you, it's just a quick-view snapshot you pull on demand.
/watch
Add to watchlist
Add a stock or crypto ticker to your personal watchlist.
Examples
/watch AAPL Add Apple to watchlist
/watch ETH Add Ethereum to watchlist
/watch Bot will ask which ticker to add
/unwatch
Remove from watchlist
Remove a ticker you no longer want to track.
Examples
/unwatch AAPL Remove Apple from watchlist
/unwatch Shows tappable list to remove from

Portfolio
7 commands
/bought
Log a buy trade
Record a real trade in your portfolio using natural language.
How to use
Just describe the trade naturally — the bot understands plain English and will confirm before logging. You can say "10 shares of NVDA at $950" or just "NVDA 10 950".
Examples
/bought 10 NVDA at 950 Log 10 shares of NVDA at $950
/bought 0.5 BTC at 97000 Log 0.5 Bitcoin at $97,000
/bought Bot will ask for trade details
/sold
Log a sell trade
Record a sale and automatically calculate your realized P&L.
How to use
Same natural-language format as /bought. The bot will match the sale to an existing open position and show you the realized gain or loss.
Examples
/sold 10 NVDA at 1010 Closes NVDA position, shows P&L
/sold AAPL 5 at 225 Partial sell — closes 5 of your shares
/positions
View open positions
See all current holdings with live P&L, exposure breakdown, and stop-loss status.
What you get
Each open position shows ticker, quantity, average entry price, current price, unrealized P&L in dollars and percent, and whether the stop-loss level has been breached. A header row summarises total capital deployed, the stock/crypto split, and your aggregate unrealized P&L across all open trades.
Examples
/positions Shows all open positions with live P&L
/history
Trade history
Review all your closed trades and their realized returns.
What you get
A log of all your completed trades — ticker, buy date, sell date, quantity, entry price, exit price, and realized P&L. Useful for tracking your actual vs. expected performance over time.
Examples
/history Shows recent closed trades
/summary
Portfolio health snapshot
One-shot view of your overall performance: win rate, total P&L, best/worst trade, streak, and all open positions with live prices.
What you get
A combined view of your all-time stats (total trades, win rate, cumulative P&L, average return per trade, best and worst outcomes, current streak) followed by every open position with its live price and percentage change from your entry. Perfect for a quick health check before the market open.
Examples
/summary Full portfolio health snapshot
/updatestop
Update a stop-loss
Move the stop-loss on an open trade — useful when trailing a winner to protect gains.
How to use
The bot will ask for the ticker, then the new stop price. After updating, the trailing-stop nudge thresholds reset so you'll get fresh breakeven and 90%-to-target alerts from the new level.
Examples
/updatestop Bot prompts for ticker then new stop price
/updatestop NVDA 118 Sets NVDA stop to $118 directly
/updatetarget
Update a profit target
Revise the target price on an open trade — extend a winner or tighten up before a risky event.
How to use
The bot will ask for the ticker, then the new target price. The trailing-stop nudge at 90% of target will recalculate automatically from the updated level.
Examples
/updatetarget Bot prompts for ticker then new target price
/updatetarget AAPL 230 Sets AAPL target to $230 directly

Paper Trading
5 commands
/paper
View paper portfolio
Check your simulated portfolio — positions, P&L, and total returns, all risk-free.
What you get
Paper trading lets you test the bot's picks without risking real money. Your paper portfolio tracks simulated buys and sells with live prices, so results reflect real market conditions.
Examples
/paper Shows simulated portfolio summary
/paper_buy
Simulate a buy
Place a paper trade buy using natural language — no real money involved.
Examples
/paper_buy 10 NVDA at 950 Simulate buying 10 NVDA at $950
/paper_buy Bot will walk you through it
/paper_sell
Simulate a sell
Close a simulated position and see your hypothetical P&L.
Examples
/paper_sell 10 NVDA at 1010 Simulate selling 10 NVDA at $1,010
/paper_sell Bot shows your paper positions to choose from
/paper_cancel
Cancel a pending paper trade
Cancel a paper trade that's been queued but not yet confirmed.
Examples
/paper_cancel Cancels any pending paper order
/paper_reset
Reset paper portfolio
Clear your entire simulated portfolio and start fresh with a clean slate.
What happens
All simulated positions and trade history are cleared. Your real portfolio is not affected. The bot will ask you to confirm before resetting.
Examples
/paper_reset Prompts confirmation, then resets paper portfolio

Settings
4 commands
/crypto
Toggle crypto picks
Turn crypto picks on or off in your daily morning message.
How to use
By default, crypto picks are included in your morning picks. Toggle them off if you only want stock picks, or back on any time.
Examples
/crypto on Enable crypto in daily picks
/crypto off Remove crypto from daily picks
/crypto Shows current setting
/timezone
Set your timezone
Choose your local timezone so daily picks and alerts arrive at the right time.
Examples
/timezone Bot shows a list of timezones to choose from
/timezone EST Set to US Eastern Time
/timezone IST Set to India Standard Time
/help
Command list
Show all available commands directly in the Telegram chat.
What you get
A quick-reference list of every command grouped by category, with brief descriptions. Tap any command in the list to run it directly. Includes a link back to this page for detailed examples.
Examples
/help Shows the full command menu in chat
/reset
Reset all settings
Restore all your preferences to their defaults. Requires confirmation.
What gets reset
Timezone, crypto toggle, and all other preferences revert to defaults. Your portfolio and trade history are not affected. The bot will ask for confirmation before proceeding.
Examples
/reset Shows confirmation prompt before resetting
