────────────────────────────────────────

today - Today's stock & crypto picks
prices - Live prices vs entry for today's picks
explain - Ask any market question or why a stock was picked
define - Plain-English explanation of any financial term
chart - Candlestick chart for any ticker
regime - Current market conditions (Bull / Bear / Caution)
accuracy - Your personal win rate: 7-day, 30-day & all-time
performance - Bot track record: win rate, avg return, vs S&P
community - All StockPulz users' performance vs S&P 500
backtest - How the strategy performed historically
earnings - Upcoming earnings dates for your positions & watchlist
size - Position sizing: how many shares to buy
bought - Log a position you entered
sold - Close a position & record P&L
positions - Open positions with live P&L & exposure
summary - Portfolio health: win rate, open P&L, risk warnings
stats - Your win rate, expectancy & P&L breakdown
history - Full trade history (open & closed)
updatestop - Move a stop-loss (e.g. to breakeven)
updatetarget - Adjust a price target
fixticker - Rename a wrongly stored ticker
dividends - Dividend yield & next ex-date for your positions
paper_buy - Simulate a buy (no real money)
paper_sell - Simulate a sell
paper_cancel - Remove a paper position without recording a sale
paper_portfolio - Virtual portfolio, cash & unrealised P&L
paper_perf - Paper trading performance vs SPY
paper_history - All past paper trades
paper_add_cash - Add cash to your paper account
paper_reset - Wipe paper portfolio and start fresh
alert - Set a price alert (e.g. NVDA above 1000)
alerts - View all your active alerts
unalert - Remove an alert
track - Track a ticker's price in your watchlist
untrack - Remove a ticker from your watchlist
settings - All preferences in one interactive panel
set_risk - Risk profile: conservative / moderate / aggressive
set_budget - Per-trade budget (e.g. stocks 200 crypto 50)
set_thresholds - Stop loss & target gain %
watch - Always include specific tickers in your picks
exclude - Exclude a sector (e.g. Energy, Utilities)
pause - Pause daily briefings
resume - Resume daily briefings
status - Check if picks are active and when the next one runs
next - When's the next scheduled pick
reset - Reset all settings to defaults
swap - Get an alternative pick for today
share - Get your invite link
feedback - Send feedback to the team
guide - Quick reference: daily schedule, /bought, /stats explained
setup - Re-run the setup wizard (budget, risk, assets)
help - Show all available commands
